About Health AI Hub

An intelligent medical diagnostic platform leveraging dual-model clinical machine learning architectures.

Our Mission

At Health AI Hub, our mission is to make preliminary healthcare information and AI clinical diagnostics accessible, comprehensive, and clear to everyone. By integrating deep learning sequence prediction and semantic parsing, we enable users to articulate symptoms in plain, free-form text and immediately receive safe, structured wellness analysis, clinical classifications, and guidance for professional consultation.

The Clinical Dataset

The platform is built and validated on the highly acclaimed academic dataset: Symptom2Disease.csv. This curated corpus contains:

24

Free Text

Unstructured Input

Unlike traditional databases that rely on strict binary symptom checklists, this dataset includes highly descriptive, natural language narratives. This replicates real-life clinical intake forms, allowing patients to describe their symptoms in their own words.

Dual Model Architecture

To maximize diagnostic precision and maintain academic transparency, Health AI Hub evaluates symptom sequences using two distinct architectures:

🤖
BiLSTM Deep Learning

Why we chose it: Symptoms are not isolated variables; they have temporal sequences and dependencies. A Bidirectional LSTM (BiLSTM) processes descriptions in both forward and backward contexts. This captures context such as "started with a fever, then a rash appeared", yielding highly contextual representation.

📊
TF-IDF + SVM Baseline

Why we chose it: Standard Support Vector Machine (SVM) combined with TF-IDF vectorization serves as a robust statistical baseline. It evaluates the raw occurrences and term frequency of clinical keywords within the corpus, mapping decision boundaries in hyperplanes. This helps validate deep learning sequence representations.
